Our Philosophy

Electrical work should be as considered as the architecture it serves.

CE Electric started with a simple frustration: too many beautiful homes were let down by wiring plans drawn up in a hurry. Panels stuffed in mechanical closets with no room to grow. Switch plates placed for convenience of the install crew, not the homeowner. We build the opposite of that.

Every project, whether it's a single-family renovation or a full commercial buildout, starts with a walk through the drawings alongside the architect or contractor. We think about how a room will actually be lived in before a single wire gets pulled. That habit is the whole business, really.

Craftsmanship Standards

  • 01

    Concealed Wiring, Always

    Every run is mapped before a wall closes, so switches, panels and low-voltage lines land exactly where the design calls for, never as an afterthought.

  • 02

    Fixtures Chosen, Not Just Installed

    We sit with clients and designers over lighting plans the way a jeweler sits with a client over a setting. Brass finishes, dimming curves and color temperature all get discussed before purchase.

  • 03

    Panels Built for the Next Twenty Years

    Extra capacity for a pool house, an EV charger, a future addition. We size service and subpanels with room to grow, not just enough to pass today's load.

  • 04

    Documentation on Every Job

    Labeled panels, as-built drawings and photos of concealed work before drywall goes up. If a wall ever needs to be opened, whoever opens it will know what they're looking at.
