What We Do

Electrical work built for houses and businesses that don't cut corners.

From copper wiring behind plaster walls to lighting scenes tied to a single switch, every job on this list gets the same attention, whether it's one remodeled kitchen or a ground-up estate.

Custom Lighting Design

We layer ambient, task and accent lighting, then wire and program them so a room reads well at every hour instead of just under one bright ceiling fixture.

Smart Home & Lighting Control

Lutron and Control4 systems set up so a whole floor dims, locks and warms up from one panel by the front door, or from a phone across town.

Panel & Service Upgrades

200 to 400 amp service upgrades for homes adding pools, home theaters or a second kitchen, sized with room to grow.

Landscape & Exterior Lighting

Low-voltage fixtures set into stone walls, tree lines and pool decks, run with wiring meant to hold up outdoors for years.

Whole-Home Generators

Standby generators sized and installed so a storm doesn't touch the wine cellar, the security system or the walk-in refrigerator.

Commercial Build-Outs

Wiring for boutique offices, restaurants and retail spaces, coordinated with architects and general contractors from permit to final walkthrough.
